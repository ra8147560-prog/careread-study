Running a session

  1. Open the participant link for the assigned condition in Chrome on the 1920 × 1080 lab monitor (or load it as a web stimulus in the eye-tracking software).
  2. Enter the participant ID and press Start. The page switches to full screen.
  3. Calibrate the eye tracker, then hand over. The flow is: scenario → fixation cross → report → fixation cross → consultation (typing, then reply) → decision.
  4. Each screen sets its own address marker (#scenario, #report, #consult-reply…) for segmenting recordings.
  5. After the decision, the data are sent to Google Sheets and the participant is taken to the Google Form.
